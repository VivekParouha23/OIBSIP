Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam

The scientist who taught a nation to dream.

A life of learning

Avul Pakir Jainulabdeen Abdul Kalam was born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, in 1931. His family was modest, and as a boy he sold newspapers to help at home. Teachers and a love of watching birds fly first pushed him toward science.

He studied aeronautical engineering and joined India’s defence research and space programmes. He worked on launch vehicles and missile technology, and colleagues came to know him as the “Missile Man of India”.

In 2002 he became the 11th President of India. He kept meeting students throughout his term, and people called him the “People’s President”.

After office he went back to teaching. He died on 27 July 2015 while giving a lecture at IIM Shillong, doing what he loved most.

Key milestones

  1. 1931Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu.
  2. 1980Led the SLV-III project that placed the Rohini satellite in orbit.
  3. 1997Awarded the Bharat Ratna, India’s highest civilian honour.
  4. 2002Became the 11th President of India.
  5. 2015Passed away in Shillong, Meghalaya.
